# Day-trading evidence and research notes

This is research context, not a guaranteed trading rule. Findings from 2026-09-25
were based on public studies and small-sample observations and should not be
treated as proof that any particular intraday strategy is profitable.

The current executable strategy is documented separately in STRATEGY.md. The
research notes support the emphasis on execution costs, volume/liquidity,
structure, and avoiding late entries, but they do not impose a fixed daily
trade count, fixed stop percentage, fixed profit target, or watchlist-only rule.

When new evidence is gathered, preserve the source, date, population and
limitations rather than turning a single study into a guarantee.
