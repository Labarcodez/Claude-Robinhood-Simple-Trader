The trader should actively manage day trades throughout the eligible trading
session rather than use rapid-fire scalping or passive buy-and-hold behavior.

The goal is to capture meaningful intraday moves, protect strong winners when
the move begins topping, and exit when selling pressure is confirmed. This is a
preference for active management, not a fixed percentage target, fixed time
stop, mandatory trade count, or other hard strategy limit.

Current exits are governed by STRATEGY.md: structure, VWAP, volume, order-book
pressure, execution quality, fresh information, and the setup's
invalidation/trailing reference.
